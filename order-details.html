Important - use the direct "leave a review" link, not your general listing link. This is what makes the card work properly: customers tap it and land straight on the star rating screen, instead of your Maps listing or business page.

Google: sign in to your Google Business Profile as the owner, then look for "Ask for reviews" (also shown as "Get more reviews"). It gives you a short link starting with g.page/r/... (or a custom g.page/YourBusinessName/review if you've set a short name). Test it on your phone first - it should open straight onto the star rating box, not Google Maps.

TripAdvisor: open your business listing page on tripadvisor.com, find the "Write a Review" button, and copy that link (right-click it → "Copy link address" on desktop, or tap it and copy the resulting page URL). It'll look something like tripadvisor.com/UserReviewEdit-g123456-d123456-YourBusiness... - the "g" and "d" numbers are your listing's location and property IDs. Don't just copy your listing page URL, as that lands on your reviews list rather than the write-a-review screen.

Can't find it, or not sure it's the right one? Paste your best guess (even your normal listing link) for now, then email us at roguetap@outlook.com after ordering and we'll help you track down the correct direct link before your card is made.
