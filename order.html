Pricing & subscription terms

Business cards (1-card and 2-card orders) include a small recurring subscription - £3.00 every 3 months for one card, or £4.50 every 3 months for two - which keeps your card's digital profile hosted and active. This is charged automatically to the card you pay with at checkout until you cancel; you can cancel any time by contacting us at roguetap@outlook.com.

Google Review and TripAdvisor cards are a single one-off payment of £19.99 with no recurring charge.

All payments are processed securely by Stripe. Rogue Tap never sees or stores your card details.
